Nothing broke. The math changed.

The model

linqd. sells outcomes, not hours: roughly 200 reps turning the best pro data in building products into revenue for clients like Lowe's.

The squeeze

Rep wages rose about 20%. Nothing else changed, and a 20% margin became zero.

The catch

Cutting reps cuts the product. The data that could make each rep more productive exists, but it sits in 15+ sources, takes 14 days to move, and reaches reps as lists rather than actions.

The turn

Three levers bring the margin back. Each one is an outcome we'll demonstrate.
